Well, look at that. [TRANSMISSION 007A - Unknown, Untitled 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enwG2O3hp9M) has hit **127 views**. That probably sounds like absolutely nothing to most YouTubers, but when the first transmission has around 35 views and most of the others are barely into double digits, it's a nice surprise.

I dug into the analytics, and almost all of those views came from YouTube recommendations, 122 from the Home page. So at least YouTube is putting this weird little project in front of people who weren't searching for it. The listening numbers are more modest; most people seem to be sampling it rather than sticking around for the whole track. No comments yet, either, so I don't know what caught their attention.

Still, that's cool as hell. Cosmic Raven is supposed to work as music _and_ as a fictional archive of transmissions from somewhere else. Maybe somebody just liked the synthwave. Maybe somebody noticed the weird numbering. Either way, somebody found a signal.

I'll keep turning the dial.
