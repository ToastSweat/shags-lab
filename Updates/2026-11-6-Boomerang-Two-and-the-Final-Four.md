# Boomerang Two and the Final Four

With BOOM1 done, I moved on to building BOOM2. The second cart gets Tom and Jerry, Looney Tunes, Huckleberry Hound, and Scooby-Doo. Between the two Boomerang carts, I now have a fairly serious supply of people being hit with things and dogs investigating property crime.

The two lineups share the same general toy-era Boomerang identity, but the shows give each cart its own personality. BOOM1 has Flintstones, Jetsons, Yogi, and Quick Draw. BOOM2 gets the cat-and-mouse warfare, Warner Bros. shorts, Huck, and the mystery gang.

The general audience commercial approach carries over, too. I don't want every animated station to have the same toy aisle for its advertising department. All the usual restrictions on repeating assets remain in place.

I also had posters made for both carts so they can sit alongside the others in Jellyfin. The finished television deserves a cover, even when the contents include commercials I went out of my way to put back in.

## What the remaining stations should actually be

While BOOM2 was building, I started narrowing down the last four carts: SCIFI1, UPN1, HBO1, and AMC1.

Some of these station names came out of the earlier planning, and I hadn't really committed to their programming yet. I don't want to download a bunch of shows just to satisfy an old placeholder.

SCIFI1 was the easiest decision. Star Trek was already the obvious anchor, and I specifically want the original series. A broader lineup with The Twilight Zone, The Outer Limits, and Quantum Leap sounded good, but I decided to simplify it further: just Star Trek TOS and the original Twilight Zone.

Alternating those two gives the cart a clear identity without needing four different shows. I already have Trek, so The Twilight Zone is the addition to collect. Exact episodes and the channel branding still need to be worked out.

The other three are less settled:

| Cart | Current direction |
|---|---|
| UPN1 | Still open. Voyager, The Sentinel, and Seven Days were suggested, but I haven't chosen the lineup. |
| HBO1 | Tales from the Crypt is the anchor I want. Dream On and The Larry Sanders Show were suggested as possible company. |
| AMC1 | The Three Stooges is the starting point. Little Rascals shorts and a classic comedy movie were suggested. |

For HBO, I also want to have some fun with adult late-night advertising, including the old hotline commercials I think I already have. Obviously, putting commercial breaks into HBO is my own fictional version of the channel. Historical accuracy can survive a small injury for the sake of a ridiculous late-night cart.

Those are directions, not finished schedules. BOOM2 was still building when I moved into this planning, and I haven't assembled any of the final four yet. Next comes checking the actual assets and making sure each station has enough of its own material to feel intentional.
