I've taken a good, long break from working on my first 'real', 'console' game. Its a virtual pet game for the Gameboy DMG, I'm making everything myself (with some assistance from AI for testing and planning).

I have a final version of the game already, but quite frankly, its boring. It's too simple, there is jsut stat bars and simple art, the music could use a real push to take it to the next level too.

I am proud of what I made, but I know it could be better.

There are so many tools to make Gameboy development easier, why shouldn't I push myself, and push the hardware?

Sure I could leave it here, but I want to publish this game, physical carts with box art, an instruction booklet, what's the point in that if i have something that makes me go 'oh yah, this is cool, but eh'

So I redesigned the minigames and the home screen, i'm playing with color modes and effects and now I have something that is really motivating me to finish.
