Site will be live at bot.helvete.com you will have to have some advanced knowledge, but not too bad, if you want to setup the app to work for you. Sign up somewhere like open router and find a text and optionally an image model you want to use, and enter the credentials in your account settings.

You may be used to other apps that let you browse bots, well this one all the power is on you to make, create, import and alter your bots. Privacy is what is in mind here, you retain all of your data, your bots, and their conversation history.

I recommend using the importer to import bots from your favorite platforms.
