## Cosmic Raven Records 
![[Art.png]]
[[Cosmic Raven Records Home]]

Some days are like this, you cant just focus on one thing, instead you bounce around from thing to thing.

A little progress in many things is better than no progress anywhere right? Whatever it takes to keep the momentum going.

It's about time for another Cosmic Raven Records post, so I consulted my list of growing notes and ideas and then ignored them all and went to bed without doing anything. If it wasn't for the last minute nothing would get done.

A few nights later when I was dozing off to sleep I was thinking about the Backrooms, new Horror, the infinite. It felt all very lovecraftian. I thought to myself that would be so cool if that's the direction I was going here, and then it hit me. Well why not?

All the pieces are in place for my narrator to look into the void and the void look back, I had a long conversation with myself and tldr I realized that the project was telling me where to go, and this is where it wants to go next. It wasn't about forcing it into a 'cool' direction, no, the story I'm telling is already about facing the infinite and why not explore that even deeper.

I don't want to give the story away, for multiple reasons, but my clear direction is even clearer and I see, with confidence, my ability to do the things I want with the story without compromising it.

And along the way I will make sure the story is being told, they way it needs to be told.
