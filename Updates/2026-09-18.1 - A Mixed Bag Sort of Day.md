## Bass VI Setup

![[Pasted image 20260919124047.png]]

I have been meaning to setup my new Bass VI, (in antigua finish ou la laa), but that would require actually taking the old factory strings off, and replacing them with the extra fan-say La Bella strings. Flatwounds, oh yah. This should give the bass a new and different sound, so I am anxious to hear it, but also anxious of wasting 60$ on string I may just hate.

Also, there is some quirks with this style of instrument produced by Fender and Squire. The bridge can rock back and forth and putting new strings on and ensuring the tuning is correct can be difficult when setup this way. A nice little hack is to throw these brass ferrules in the bridge post holes, the inside and outside diameters are a perfect fit and the bridge should stay in tune way better.

![[Pasted image 20260919124124.png]]

And what do you know? It does! that was worth about 15 cents and my time. But the string? That's another story. 

You know, I don't hate them, I also don't think I love them. They are their own thing, they do in fact have a distinct and i would call muted sound. It sounds interesting distorted but the true beauty comes in on clean. The Bass sings, the notes are so clear and clean, this is where the world of difference is. Playing something like Hey You but Pink Floyd, the strings really do the heavy lifting for the song. I do like this tone, it's not an all use case sort of thing, but I see the appeal. If the Bass VI was fretless, then I think this would be a no brainer... and maybe that's an idea for the future.

![[Pasted image 20260919124201.png]]

I did not intonate of adjust the truss rod, the bass was playing so well and the action felt good, so i left it as is. Later I will install strap locks so the strap does never comes off accidentally and send my precious, hideous bass flying into harms way or left solely to the whims of gravity.

Oh, Penny. Why that name for a bass? Well, all instruments need a name. To me, it kind of looks like a moldy Piece of Bread? Work with me here, so Penny is short for Penicillin? Dad joke levels of bad. Go ahead, groan, I'll wait.

One observation I had was the tuners are still shit. I don't find them to be this bass on my other Squire Bass VI, so that's a little disappointing. I think in the near future I will replace the tuners will better quality ones, but that is a project for another day...
