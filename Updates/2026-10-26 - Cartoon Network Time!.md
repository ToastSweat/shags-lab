I made cart/channel number 6! The first of the cartoon network channels.

The quality of the commercials and shows really jumped but they still retain that retro charm.

Mostly I cant wait to watch some shows I haven't seen in years.

Nick got Doug, Hey Arnold, Cat Dog, Angry Beavers, Ah Real Monsters.

Now on Cartoon Network we're going to get some greats like Dexter's Lab, Powerpuff Girls, Ed, Edd and Eddy and more (I haven't decided yet)

Making the carts is starting to feel routine, the commands are still a PITA but it's making me realize that if I want this more of a polished product for people to use I may have to redesign. So considering that as a final wrap up to the project.

For now I will continue to make them and play them, the tool is getting there, functionality is 95% of the way there, maybe a UI an UX redesign is worth it.
