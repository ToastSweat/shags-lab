# Fretless

A fretless instrument has no metal frets stopping the strings at fixed pitches. Your finger position does that job instead. That makes smooth slides and tiny pitch changes easy, but it also means your ears and hands have to agree on exactly where each note belongs. Lines on the fingerboard, when present, are guides rather than frets.

[[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny]] prompted some fretless daydreaming after its flatwound-string experiment. The clean sound made the idea appealing, but the project home says Shag does not want to ruin the nice fretboard. It is an idea, not a completed conversion.

There is already a real example in the collection: the [[Bass - Ibanez SRAS7 Home|Ibanez SRAS7]] combines fretted and fretless sections on one neck, with different strings and pickups for each. That is a purpose-built hybrid, rather than an ordinary bass with a few frets missing.
