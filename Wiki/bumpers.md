# Bumpers

Bumpers are the short bits of television between the bigger bits: “we'll be right back,” “now back to the show,” a quick character gag, or a branded transition. They make a commercial break feel attached to a channel instead of like somebody dropped unrelated files into the queue.

They are a surprisingly important part of [[My Mini Retro Television Channels Home]]. The aim is not simply to store old episodes on USB drives; it is to recreate the rhythm of turning on a television in a particular era.

The October 11, 2026 update describes a deliberately lean sequence: show, commercial, matched or generic network bumper, next show. Earlier experiments had too many transitions and branding for the wrong programs. The builder now prefers a bumper matching the upcoming show when one is available, with the [[Wiki/SNICK|SNICK]] cart providing a useful test.

Compared with [[Wiki/idents|idents]], which primarily identify a network, bumpers primarily connect or frame the programming. The same clip may serve both purposes. Useful neighbors are [[Wiki/Nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]], [[Wiki/Cartoon Network|Cartoon Network]], and [[2026-10-11 - I Built Nickelodeon On A Tiny TV]].
