# DBZS Zombie Survival RPG

DBZS Zombie Survival RPG is one of the servers Shagwrath names in his personal [[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]] history. That note establishes that he worked on it, but does not describe his exact role, the original server's dates, or its systems.

There is also a later breadcrumb: the [[PGN - Master Timeline]] records a July 26, 2023 message about working with Nick on a FiveM zombie server, nearly having a core to build on, and bringing DBZS back. That documents a revival effort, not proof that a finished replacement launched.

The distinction matters because MTA and FiveM are different multiplayer platforms. Reusing the name does not make them one uninterrupted server. The vault does not yet explain how much of the older design carried over, or what happened after the 2023 work.
