# Truss Rod

A truss rod is an adjustable metal rod inside an instrument's neck. It helps balance the pull of the strings and controls the neck's small lengthwise curve, usually called relief. That curve gives vibrating strings room to move without requiring every string to sit miles above the fingerboard.

It is related to [[Wiki/Action|Action]], but it is not a general-purpose string-height knob. The [[Wiki/Bridge|Bridge]], nut, frets, and neck condition all matter too. If something feels wrong, finding the cause beats randomly tightening the rod. Never force an adjustment that will not turn normally.

In [[Bass VI - Penny - First Time Setup|Penny's first setup]], Shag left the truss rod alone because the bass was playing well and the action felt good. That is the useful lesson here: changing strings does not automatically mean the neck needs adjusting. Check the result at the intended [[Wiki/Tuning|Tuning]] before deciding there is a problem.
