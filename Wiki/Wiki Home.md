# Wiki

Something in a post made you go “what the hell is that?” This is the place for that.

These are short explanations of the tools, instruments, games, and old community lore that keep turning up in the Lab. Enough to follow the story without needing a computer-science degree or a guitar tech on standby.

Start with [[Wiki/Video Games|Video Games]] for the gaming side, [[Wiki/Obsidian|Obsidian]] and [[Wiki/Quartz|Quartz]] for how this site works, or [[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]] for the people behind a lot of the bad decisions. The actual builds live in [[Projects/Projects Home|Projects]], and the day-to-day rambling lives in [[Updates/Updates Home|Updates]].
