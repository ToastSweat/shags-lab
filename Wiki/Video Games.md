# Video Games

Video games are both something to play and, around here, an excuse to build things. A shared world can turn into a server project; a little puzzle can turn into a Discord Activity; a joke about a character can turn into a Game Boy cartridge.

## Playing together

[[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]] is the social thread connecting much of the vault. [[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]] is central to the earlier history, while [[Wiki/Valheim|Valheim]] and [[Wiki/Dune Awakening|Dune Awakening]] represent the recurring habit of moving into another shared survival world. [[Wiki/Star Wars Battlefront II|Star Wars Battlefront II]] shows the other favorite habit: giving an old game a new server and far more supporting software than anyone originally planned.

## Making games

[[Wiki/Safe Cracker|Safe Cracker]] is the author's daily combination puzzle. [[Garths Game DMG Home|Garth's Game DMG]] turns [[Wiki/Garth Algar|Garth Algar]] into a virtual pet on actual Game Boy hardware. [[Projects/Hermes/VR Lightsaber Trainer/VR Lightsaber Trainer|VR Lightsaber Trainer]] explores a different kind of interaction through [[Wiki/VR|VR]]. These are separate projects with different stages of development, not a single finished catalogue.

For the history behind the friend group, start with [[PGN Discord Archive Home]]. For ongoing work rather than topic summaries, use [[Projects Home]] and the dated updates.
