# Nico Robin

Nico Robin is a member of the Straw Hat crew in *One Piece*. She is an archaeologist whose interest in the world's buried history makes her a rather different kind of pirate from the usual treasure-hunting stereotype.

Her appearance in this vault is much more practical—and sillier. In [[2026-09-17 - I Built A Booru Librarian]], the author jokes that Robin is going to rob him of all his terabytes while describing an image-download collection that had already grown very large.

That is the documented local connection: a character reference inside the [[Booru Image Downloader Home|image-downloader project]], not a claim that she is part of [[Wiki/Cosmic Raven Universe|Cosmic Raven Universe]] or one of the author's own creations. The note does not provide a broader personal history with *One Piece*, so there is no need to invent one.
