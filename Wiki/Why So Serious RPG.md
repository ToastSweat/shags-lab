# Why So Serious RPG

Why So Serious RPG, abbreviated WSS in the PGN history, is a [[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]] server that sits between the author's earlier SAES/Helvete period and the creation of PGN.

In his personal account, Shagwrath leaves [[Wiki/San Andreas Emergency Services|San Andreas Emergency Services]] after a falling-out and joins friends on WSS. After another falling-out, he and other friends leave to make [[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]] and the [[Wiki/PGN RPG|PGN RPG]] server. That is a useful first-person sequence, but it does not establish exact dates, a complete account of the disagreements, or the server's wider history.

WSS also appears in the Discord archive's later recollections of old MTA culture, scripts, and friendships. A retrospective song includes it in the lineage “Hell Soldiers → Helvete → WSS → PGN.” As the archive notes, the song is oral history, not a contemporary founding document.
