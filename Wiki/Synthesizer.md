# Synthesizer

A synthesizer makes sound electronically, rather than relying on a vibrating string or a blown tube. You can shape the sound itself, not just choose which note to play. It might imitate a familiar instrument, make a thick bass line, or produce a noise that has absolutely no acoustic equivalent.

The instrument collection groups several electronic boxes under “Synth,” but they do not all work the same way. A sampler plays recordings; a sequencer arranges notes or events. One device can combine those jobs with actual synthesis.

The [[Synth - Teenage Engineering EP–40 Riddim Home|EP–40 Riddim]] is a useful local example because its notes describe sampling, sequencing, and an onboard synth engine. The [[Synth - Teenage Engineering EP–1320 Medieval Home|EP–1320 Medieval]] leans on a library of medieval sounds and sampling. The folder label is handy shorthand, not a promise that every box inside it generates sound in exactly the same way.
