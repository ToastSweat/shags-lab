# Star Wars Galactic Racer

*Star Wars: Galactic Racer* is a racing game set in the Outer Rim after the Empire's fall. The official description centers on the Galactic League, an unsanctioned circuit where the racer Shade pursues glory and revenge. Racing craft, hazardous routes, rivalries, and a story-driven campaign put the focus on speed rather than Jedi powers.

The local connection is brief but direct: [[2026-10-08 - Slow Day]] ends with the author heading off to play it. That establishes a game mentioned in the diary, not a review, a PGN server project, or evidence of how much time the group spent with it.

The title is verified by the [official Star Wars game page](https://www.starwars.com/games-apps/star-wars-galactic-racer) and the [official game website](https://starwarsgalacticracer.com/). This article does not infer a release date from the diary entry.
