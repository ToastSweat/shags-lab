# Tuning

Tuning means setting each string to its intended pitch. It can also mean the overall arrangement of notes across the strings. The usual guitar layout is E–A–D–G–B–E; a [[Wiki/Bass VI|Bass VI]] uses those same intervals one octave lower. Same note names, substantially more rumble.

Staying in tune involves more than turning the tuners. New strings need to settle, and moving hardware or strings catching at contact points can make the pitch wander. [[Wiki/Intonation|Intonation]] is a separate check: whether notes remain in tune farther up the neck after the open strings are correct.

[[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny's]] first setup tackled [[Wiki/Bridge|Bridge]] movement with brass [[Wiki/Ferrules|Ferrules]] and tried flatwound strings. The account reports better stability but still complains about the tuners, leaving replacements for later. Meanwhile, [[Guitar - Charles Home|Charles]] was bought partly with possible drop tuning in mind. That is a proposed use, not a documented completed retuning.
