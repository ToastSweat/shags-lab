# Cartoon Network

Cartoon Network is a television channel built around animation: original cartoons, acquired shows, and the branding that makes the whole schedule feel like one place. For this vault, the interesting version is the late-1990s and early-2000s channel, rather than a guide to its current programming.

It is one of the planned stations in [[My Mini Retro Television Channels Home]]. The September 19, 2026 update sketches carts with shows such as Dexter's Laboratory and The Powerpuff Girls. The October 11 update puts Cartoon Network next after the Nickelodeon phase, with a first cart aimed at the Powerhouse era.

The small pieces matter as much as the episodes. [[Wiki/idents|idents]], [[Wiki/bumpers|bumpers]], commercials, and sensible transitions are what turn a folder of cartoons into a convincing fake broadcast. Shorts also need to be grouped into show-sized blocks so the builder does not interrupt every segment with another break.

See [[2026-10-11 - I Built Nickelodeon On A Tiny TV]] for that workflow, plus [[Wiki/Nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]] and [[Wiki/Kids' WB|Kids' WB]] for the neighboring channels in the project.
