# Bridge Post

A bridge post is one of the supports holding a [[Wiki/Bridge|Bridge]] above the body of an instrument. On the vintage-style [[Wiki/Bass VI|Bass VI]] bridge discussed here, the posts sit inside metal-lined holes. The bridge can rock back and forth as the vibrato changes string tension. That movement is part of the design, not automatically a broken part.

On [[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny]], the loose movement made restringing and keeping the bridge positioned a nuisance. Brass [[Wiki/Ferrules|Ferrules]] were fitted into the post holes to restrict that rocking, as described in the [[Bass VI - Penny - First Time Setup|first setup]] write-up.

That is a modification to this particular bridge arrangement, not advice to jam any sleeve into any guitar. Fit matters, and restricting movement changes how the bridge works with the vibrato. The aim here was a more stable position and less [[Wiki/Tuning|Tuning]] frustration.
