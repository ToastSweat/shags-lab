# MIDI

**MIDI** is a way for musical instruments and software to exchange performance instructions. It can say “play this note now,” how strongly to play it, and when to stop. It isn't the recorded sound itself: the same MIDI part can drive a piano, a synthesizer, or a drum kit, depending on what receives it.

That distinction matters in the [[Projects/Reaper Drum Humanizer/Reaper Drum Humanizer Home|Drum Humanizer]]. I write drum parts in Guitar Pro, export the MIDI, and process it in [[Wiki/REAPER|REAPER]]. The script changes note timing and velocity—the strength attached to a hit—rather than editing an audio recording.

Perfectly timed instructions can make a convincing drum sound feel suspiciously robotic. The humanizer gives the imaginary drummer different hands and feet instead of simply making every note randomly wrong. A very elaborate way to let the machine loosen up a little.
