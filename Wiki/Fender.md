# Fender

Fender is the instrument company behind familiar designs such as the Stratocaster, Telecaster, Precision Bass, and Jazz Bass. Around this vault, its relevance is often less about the logo and more about the design ideas that keep showing up on instruments and their hardware.

The [[Wiki/Bass VI|Bass VI]] is one of those ideas: guitar-style string intervals, bass-register [[Wiki/Tuning|Tuning]], and a compact feel compared with many full-size basses. [[Wiki/Squier|Squier]] is Fender's more affordable instrument brand, and both [[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny]] and [[Bass VI - The Loaf Pincher Home|The Loaf Pincher]] are documented as Classic Vibe Bass VIs.

That relationship explains why a note can talk about a Fender-style [[Wiki/Bridge|Bridge]] while the actual instrument says Squier on the headstock. It also explains the shared setup quirks behind Penny's [[Wiki/Ferrules|bridge-stabilizing modification]]. The brand and the particular instrument are related, but they are not interchangeable names.
