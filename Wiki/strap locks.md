# Strap Locks

Strap locks keep a guitar or bass strap from slipping off the instrument's strap buttons. Some replace the buttons and attach a matching locking piece to the strap; simpler retainers sit over a normal button. The shared purpose is boring in the best way: keeping an expensive instrument off the floor.

[[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny's]] setup account lists strap locks as a later job, after the string change and [[Wiki/Bridge|Bridge]] work. They were planned protection for the “precious, hideous” bass, not a modification the write-up says was already finished.

Locks are only as secure as their installation. A loose button screw, a badly fitted strap fitting, or an incompletely engaged latch can still let things go wrong. Check them before trusting the strap with the full weight. They do not change [[Wiki/Tuning|Tuning]], [[Wiki/Action|Action]], or tone; they just make standing up with the instrument less exciting.
