# Cosmic Raven Universe

Cosmic Raven Universe is a worldbuilding topic linked from the vault's creative projects. The clearest developed piece is [[Cosmic Raven Records Home|Cosmic Raven Records]]: a fictional archive of music recovered through a homemade receiver that seems to tune into other possibilities.

The songs and finished videos are real creative work; the receiver, recovered histories, and alternate realities are the fiction wrapped around them. The narrator does not have a reliable explanation of the machine. That uncertainty is part of the story, not a gap that needs filling with a grand theory of the multiverse.

[[Garths Game DMG Home|Garth's Game DMG]] also links here, but the existing notes do not explain exactly how its virtual-pet setting fits the wider universe. A link is not enough to declare shared characters, timelines, or rules. Those connections still need an authored explanation.

For the established premise, start with [[2026-09-20 - Cosmic Raven Records]]. The [[Cosmic Raven Records - Archive Index]] collects the transmissions themselves. This page can grow into a setting guide as more of the connections are actually written down.
