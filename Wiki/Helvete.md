# Helvete

Helvete is an old [[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]] group identity that kept surviving long after any one server era. In the local history it sits between [[Wiki/Hell Soldiers|Hell Soldiers]] and the move to [[Wiki/Why So Serious RPG|Why So Serious RPG]], before [[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]] formed.

The personal MTA note supplies that sequence. A later PGN song repeats “Hell Soldiers → Helvete → WSS → PGN,” but the archive correctly treats the song as retrospective lore, not a precise founding record.

What makes Helvete interesting is how often the name comes back. The [[PGN - Master Timeline]] preserves a 2018 message about wanting a tattoo to remember the MTA years, a 2019 suggestion to rebrand PGN, a 2020 SAES revival, and later restaurant-themed roleplay. By 2024 the timeline records Helvete becoming official again on SAES. The September 2026 archive update also connects the name with `helvete.pizza`.

So “bring Helvete back” can mean reviving a group, reusing a logo, or just carrying an old shared joke into a new project. [[PGN - Lore and Running Jokes]] follows those changing meanings without pretending they were one continuous organization.
