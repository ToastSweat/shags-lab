# Safe Cracker

Safe Cracker is the author's number-guessing puzzle game, not a general article about opening real safes. In its Discord Activity version, everyone gets the same daily four-digit combination. Each guess receives HIGHER or LOWER feedback; fewer guesses wins, with time used to break ties.

The [[2026-09-28 - I Downloaded Ten Years of Discord and Accidentally Became the Clan Historian|PGN archive update]] traces the concept back to the MTA/SAES days, through a Unity rebuild, a considered Game Boy version, and a Steam game, before the Discord adaptation. Those are stages in the project's history, not interchangeable editions.

[[2026-10-05.1 - Safe Cracker Is Live]] reports the Discord Activity's verified launch, including authentication, saved results, streaks, leaderboards, and sharing. The later [[2026-10-21 - I Made Photoshop Build My Discord Activity]] describes work on the visual redesign and a Photoshop-driven export workflow. A redesign in progress should not be confused with every proposed Phase 2 feature being shipped.
