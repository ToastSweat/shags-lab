# Ferrules

Ferrules are small sleeves or collars used to line a hole, support a part, or provide a tidy bearing surface. On guitars, the word often means the little metal cups that hold string ball ends in a string-through body. It does not always mean that particular part, though.

In [[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny's]] setup notes, the ferrules are brass sleeves fitted into the [[Wiki/Bridge Post|bridge-post]] holes. They take up the extra space that lets the vintage-style [[Wiki/Bridge|Bridge]] rock. The goal was to hold the bridge in a more consistent position and reduce [[Wiki/Tuning|Tuning]] hassle during string changes and playing.

The [[Bass VI - Penny - First Time Setup|first setup]] links to the account of that inexpensive modification and its reported improvement. That result belongs to this instrument and those well-fitting sleeves; it is not a claim that every ferrule fits every bridge, or that all rocking bridges need fixing.
