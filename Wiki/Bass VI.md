# Bass VI

A Bass VI is a six-string instrument tuned E–A–D–G–B–E, one octave below a standard guitar. The lowest four strings cover the familiar four-string bass tuning, with two higher strings added. It is not the same layout as a typical six-string bass, which usually adds both a lower and a higher string to a four-string bass.

The classic [[Wiki/Fender|Fender]] and [[Wiki/Squier|Squier]] design uses a 30-inch scale: shorter than many basses, longer than most guitars. That makes it a fun middle ground for bass lines, chords, and low, sludgy guitar-style playing.

The local collection includes [[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny]], [[Bass VI - The Loaf Pincher Home|The Loaf Pincher]], and the multiscale [[Bass VI - Ibanez SRC6MS Home|Ibanez SRC6MS]]. Penny keeps its vibrato; The Loaf Pincher has a hardtail conversion. Same general territory, different approaches to the hardware and feel.
