# Idents

An ident is a short clip that tells you which television channel or programming block you are watching. Sometimes it is just a logo and a sound; sometimes it is a tiny animated sketch. Its main job is identity: “this is Nickelodeon,” rather than “here is the next episode.”

In [[My Mini Retro Television Channels Home]], idents help sell the illusion that a USB drive is a little television station. A cart can open with a network ID, then use other branding between shows. Without those pieces, the result feels more like a video playlist than a broadcast.

The October 11, 2026 update also shows why matching the branding pool matters. A [[Wiki/SNICK|SNICK]] cart initially configured as regular Nickelodeon received the wrong IDs, even though the software was following its configuration correctly.

Idents overlap with [[Wiki/bumpers|bumpers]], but the terms emphasize different jobs: an ident names the channel; a bumper bridges a break or program transition. One clip can do both. See [[Wiki/Nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]], [[Wiki/Cartoon Network|Cartoon Network]], [[Wiki/Kids' WB|Kids' WB]], and [[Wiki/FOX|FOX]] for the local channel plans.
