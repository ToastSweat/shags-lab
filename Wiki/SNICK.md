# SNICK

SNICK, short for Saturday Night Nickelodeon, was Nickelodeon's Saturday-night programming block. Its particular nighttime identity is the important part here: it should feel related to [[Wiki/Nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]], but not like a regular daytime cart with a different label.

In [[My Mini Retro Television Channels Home]], `SNICK1` is the fifth cart in the first Nickelodeon phase. The October 11, 2026 update describes a build alternating Are You Afraid of the Dark? and Ren & Stimpy, with SNICK branding, old commercials, and a closing sequence. That is this project's chosen lineup, not a claim to reproduce every historical SNICK schedule.

It became a useful test of the builder. Configuring it as regular `NICK` initially selected the wrong branding. Giving it its own pool made show-matched [[Wiki/bumpers|bumpers]] work properly. Ren & Stimpy shorts also needed combining into show-sized blocks so a commercial did not land between two halves.

The update says SNICK1 was building at the time of writing; it should not be read as a later playback verification. See [[2026-10-11 - I Built Nickelodeon On A Tiny TV]] and [[Wiki/idents|idents]].
