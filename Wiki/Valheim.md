# Valheim

Valheim is a Viking-inspired survival and building game. Gathering materials, making a base, exploring dangerous places, and fighting bosses are the basic loop. Played with friends, the shared settlement often becomes as memorable as the progression.

That makes it a natural fit for [[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]]: another persistent place for the group to inhabit, maintain, abandon, and eventually return to. The [[PGN - Game Eras and Server Genealogy]] describes recurring interest across several years and a substantial server/settlement phase in 2025. Its archive references are evidence of conversation and activity, not a tally of hours played.

The game is still part of the immediate diary in [[2026-10-08 - Slow Day]], where the author says the friend group has been playing Valheim and that it has taken time away from other projects. That is a small but useful reminder that the point of all these game servers is sometimes simply playing the game.
