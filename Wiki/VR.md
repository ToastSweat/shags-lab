# VR

Virtual reality, or VR, uses a headset to place your view inside a computer-generated environment. Head and controller tracking let looking around and moving your hands become part of the interaction, instead of only pressing buttons while watching a flat screen.

A game being available for PC does not automatically make it a VR game, and owning a VR game does not guarantee the same experience on every headset. Software support, controls, connection method, and the computer doing the work all matter.
