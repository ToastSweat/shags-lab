# Antigua

Antigua is a distinctive [[Wiki/Fender|Fender]] finish: a pale center surrounded by a darker, shaded edge, often with a matching-looking pickguard. It has a very particular vintage look. Depending on the instrument and the lighting, that can land somewhere between elegant old furniture and slightly suspicious food.

That second interpretation explains [[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny]]. The finish reminded Shag of moldy bread, so Penny became short for penicillin. Not Shrek. There was apparently a naming process.

Antigua describes the appearance, not a different kind of [[Wiki/Bass VI|Bass VI]] or a special [[Wiki/Tuning|Tuning]]. Penny's sound and feel come from the instrument and its setup, including the flatwound string experiment documented in [[Bass VI - Penny - First Time Setup|First Time Setup]]. The unusual finish is the reason for the name, not the reason the notes sound different.
