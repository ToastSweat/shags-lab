# Valve

Valve is the video-game company behind Steam and games such as Half-Life and Portal. Steam is its PC game storefront and platform; Valve's work also includes gaming hardware and virtual-reality projects.

Here the company is mostly relevant through the author's [[Wiki/VR|VR]] experience. [[2026-10-07 - Steam Frame. So Lame.]] directly addresses Valve while complaining about the [[Wiki/Steam Frame|Steam Frame]] experience and comparing it with an older [[Wiki/Oculus Rift CV1|Oculus Rift CV1]]. The post is a personal, dated reaction, not a review of every Valve product or a verified list of hardware capabilities.

The next step in that diary entry is more important to the local project history than the complaint itself: trying to build a lightsaber combat game with AI assistance. [[Projects/Hermes/VR Lightsaber Trainer/VR Lightsaber Trainer|VR Lightsaber Trainer]] is the resulting project page.

For other gaming topics, see [[Wiki/Video Games|Video Games]]. [[Wiki/Star Wars Battlefront II|Star Wars Battlefront II]] belongs to a separate server project in the vault; using a Steam copy of a game does not mean Valve developed that game.
