# Kids' WB

Kids' WB was a children's television programming block associated with The WB, remembered for cartoons and the energetic branding around them. It is a programming block, not simply another name for Cartoon Network, even where shows or audiences overlap.

In this vault it is one of the planned identities for [[My Mini Retro Television Channels Home]]. The September 19, 2026 update lists `KWB01` and `KWB02` in the proposed USB cartridge lineup and mentions Kids' WB shows and commercial archives among the available material. The October 4 update still has its branding work ahead.

That gives the project a direction, but not a documented final schedule. The notes do not yet establish which episodes went onto each cart or whether those carts were completed. Keeping that distinction avoids turning a planning list into an imaginary finished broadcast.

As with [[Wiki/Nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]], [[Wiki/Cartoon Network|Cartoon Network]], and [[Wiki/FOX|FOX]], the identity depends on the small pieces: period-appropriate commercials, [[Wiki/idents|idents]], [[Wiki/bumpers|bumpers]], and transitions that actually fit the selected shows.
