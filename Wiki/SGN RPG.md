# SGN RPG

SGN RPG is one of the additional servers Shagwrath says he worked on in his personal [[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]] history. That is the main evidence currently available for this particular entry.

The note does not expand the initials, describe his work, or give dates, features, or an outcome. The PGN archive separately contains a 2017 question about whether SGN had an ARK server, but that is not enough to equate the ARK reference with this RPG server or reconstruct its organization.

For now, this page is a small history marker rather than a finished server profile. Older screenshots, scripts, forum posts, or an authored recollection could fill it out without guessing.
