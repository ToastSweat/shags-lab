# Nickelodeon

Nickelodeon is a children's television network, often shortened to Nick. Here, `NICK` is also a working label for the retro-TV project's regular Nickelodeon branding pool, so the all-caps form in older notes is a project shorthand rather than a separate channel.

[[My Mini Retro Television Channels Home]] uses USB drives as pretend broadcast cartridges. The October 11, 2026 update describes four regular Nickelodeon carts, `NICK1` through `NICK4`, using shows such as Doug, Rugrats, Hey Arnold!, Rocko's Modern Life, Angry Beavers, CatDog, and Aaahh!!! Real Monsters. The goal is the feeling of watching television, not just selecting a favorite episode.

That means commercials, [[Wiki/idents|idents]], and [[Wiki/bumpers|bumpers]] belong in the sequence too—but not so many that they overwhelm the cartoons. The builder's revised rhythm became show, commercial, bumper, next show.

[[Wiki/SNICK|SNICK]] gets its own branding pool and cart rather than borrowing the regular Nick material. See [[2026-10-11 - I Built Nickelodeon On A Tiny TV]] for the first five-cart phase, and [[Wiki/Cartoon Network|Cartoon Network]] and [[Wiki/Kids' WB|Kids' WB]] for other planned stations.
