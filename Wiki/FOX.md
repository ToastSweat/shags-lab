# FOX

FOX is an American broadcast television network. In this vault the name comes up mainly as part of the retro-TV project, where it needs a little more precision than a logo on a USB drive.

The September 19, 2026 cart plan includes a `FOX96` cartridge. The same update mentions Fox Kids commercial material, while the October 4 update lists FOX among the remaining branding pools to sort. These are plans for recreating a period viewing experience, not evidence that every FOX-branded clip belongs to the same schedule.

Fox Kids was a children's programming block associated with FOX, not a synonym for the entire network. A convincing cart should match the chosen shows, [[Wiki/idents|idents]], commercials, and [[Wiki/bumpers|bumpers]] to the intended block and era rather than mix them indiscriminately.

The existing notes do not yet give FOX96 a final lineup or record its completion. See [[My Mini Retro Television Channels Home]] for the project, alongside [[Wiki/Kids' WB|Kids' WB]], [[Wiki/Cartoon Network|Cartoon Network]], and [[Wiki/Nickelodeon|Nickelodeon]].
