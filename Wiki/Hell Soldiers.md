# Hell Soldiers

Hell Soldiers is an early group name in the local [[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]] history. The author's personal MTA note writes it as “Hell's Soldiers” and places it before making [[Wiki/Helvete|Helvete]], during the story that begins on [[Wiki/San Andreas Emergency Services|San Andreas Emergency Services]] in 2014.

A later PGN retrospective song gives the lineage as “Hell Soldiers → Helvete → WSS → PGN.” The [[PGN - The Epic History|archive history]] explicitly labels that song's sequence as oral tradition rather than a precise contemporary chronology. The personal note supports the broad connection, but neither source supplies a full roster, exact founding date, or explanation of the name change.

For now, this page preserves the name without inventing the missing era around it. Related: [[Wiki/Why So Serious RPG|Why So Serious RPG]], [[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]], and [[2026-09-28 - I Downloaded Ten Years of Discord and Accidentally Became the Clan Historian]].
