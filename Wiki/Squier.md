# Squier

Squier is Fender's more affordable instrument brand. The spelling is Squier, even though “Squire” is an easy mistake and appears in some older project notes. It is a brand name, not a separate type of guitar or a special feature.

The important local examples are the Classic Vibe [[Wiki/Bass VI|Bass VI]] instruments: [[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny]] and [[Bass VI - The Loaf Pincher Home|The Loaf Pincher]]. They follow the older [[Wiki/Fender|Fender]] design, including its distinctive controls and vintage-style bridge arrangement, but they have ended up with different modifications.

Penny keeps its vibrato and received brass [[Wiki/Ferrules|Ferrules]] to limit [[Wiki/Bridge|Bridge]] rocking. The Loaf Pincher has a hardtail conversion instead. Penny's setup account also complains about its tuners while saying the other Squier Bass VI's tuners were less troublesome. That is a comparison between two actual instruments, not a verdict that every Squier needs the same replacement parts.
