# Multi Theft Auto

Multi Theft Auto, usually MTA, is a multiplayer modification for Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas. Community servers can add their own scripts, activities, and social rules, so the same game becomes a platform for very different kinds of shared worlds.

Here it is also the starting point for much of [[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]]'s history. The account below is the author's own recollection; it is preserved as written rather than replaced with a guessed chronology. “Hell's Soldiers” refers to the history topic collected at [[Wiki/Hell Soldiers|Hell Soldiers]].

## Personal history

My personal involvement with Multi Theft auto start in 2014 on the [[Wiki/San Andreas Emergency Services|San Andreas Emergency Services]] Server, SAES:RPG.

After Hell's Soldiers and making [[Wiki/Helvete|Helvete]], I had a falling out with the SAES clan, and left to join some friends on the [[Wiki/Why So Serious RPG|Why So Serious RPG]] server.

After another falling out there me and some other friends left to make [[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]] and the [[Wiki/PGN RPG|PGN RPG]] server, and another group of people went out to make Nightlife RPG, then Excision, then Nightlife RPG again. Then we got back together during covid to make [[Wiki/ANAL RPG|ANAL RPG]], not my finest name choice but I am proud of what we did.

I have worked on a few other servers, including [[Wiki/SGN RPG|SGN RPG]] and [[Wiki/DBZS Zombie Survival RPG|DBZS Zombie Survival RPG]], boy I am getting tired of typing RPG.
