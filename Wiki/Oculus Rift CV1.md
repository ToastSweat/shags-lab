# Oculus Rift CV1

The Oculus Rift CV1 is the original consumer Rift headset for PC virtual reality. It belongs to the generation of [[Wiki/VR|VR]] hardware designed around a connected computer, rather than being a general name for every Oculus or Meta headset.

Its local role is a point of comparison. In [[2026-10-07 - Steam Frame. So Lame.]], the author remembers buying VR games for a Rift CV1 and having them continue to work well as the headset aged. That makes the frustration with the newer [[Wiki/Steam Frame|Steam Frame]] setup easier to understand: the expectation was access to an existing library, not starting over with only a few games that appeared verified.

That update is an account of the author's experience, not a compatibility survey. It does not establish which games were tested, what settings were used, or how every headset compares.

The frustration becomes the starting point for a homemade lightsaber combat experiment. See [[Projects/Hermes/VR Lightsaber Trainer/VR Lightsaber Trainer|VR Lightsaber Trainer]] for that project and [[Wiki/Valve|Valve]] for the company referenced in the Steam Frame post.
