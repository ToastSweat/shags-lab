# San Andreas Emergency Services

San Andreas Emergency Services, referred to in the author's notes as SAES:RPG, is a [[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]] server and community. For this vault, its importance is less a list of game systems and more the friendships and projects that grew out of playing there.

Shagwrath's personal MTA history starts on SAES in 2014. It then mentions Hell's Soldiers, making [[Wiki/Helvete|Helvete]], and eventually leaving after a falling-out to join friends on [[Wiki/Why So Serious RPG|Why So Serious RPG]]. That is the author's account of his own route through the community, not a complete history of SAES.

SAES also returns in later PGN records. The [[PGN - Master Timeline]] documents a Helvete revival in 2020 and another official-group milestone in 2024. The September 28, 2026 archive update traces [[Wiki/Safe Cracker|Safe Cracker]] back to a concept tied to the old MTA/SAES days.

Those later returns are worth keeping separate from the original 2014 experience. The vault does not yet supply enough detail to map every membership period or server change.
