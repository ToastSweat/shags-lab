# Steam Frame

Steam Frame is the headset discussed in the author's October 7, 2026 post, [[2026-10-07 - Steam Frame. So Lame.]]. This entry records its place in the vault rather than trying to serve as a hardware specification sheet.

In that post, the author describes frustration with how few games in an existing [[Wiki/VR|VR]] library appeared verified and with the PC-streaming experience. The comparison is with an older [[Wiki/Oculus Rift CV1|Oculus Rift CV1]], remembered as working well with purchased games even as it aged.

Those are dated, personal observations. The post's mention of two verified games describes that library and moment; it is not a platform-wide compatibility count. The notes do not establish tested titles, software versions, or a reproducible performance comparison, and no headset specifications are asserted here.

The useful local consequence is creative: the author decides to try making a lightsaber combat game with AI assistance rather than only complain about the available games. That becomes [[Projects/Hermes/VR Lightsaber Trainer/VR Lightsaber Trainer|VR Lightsaber Trainer]]. Related: [[Wiki/Valve|Valve]] and [[Wiki/Video Games|Video Games]].
