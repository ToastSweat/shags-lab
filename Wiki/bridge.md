# Bridge

The bridge is the hardware near the body end of the strings that supports them at the right height and spacing. Its little string supports, called saddles, often let you adjust [[Wiki/Action|Action]] and [[Wiki/Intonation|Intonation]]. Depending on the instrument, the strings may anchor at the bridge itself or at a separate tailpiece behind it.

A bridge does not have to move. The vintage-style [[Wiki/Bass VI|Bass VI]] arrangement can rock on its [[Wiki/Bridge Post|posts]] as the vibrato works, while a fixed bridge stays put. Neither design is automatically the winner; they just come with different setup considerations.

[[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny]] keeps its vibrato but uses brass [[Wiki/Ferrules|Ferrules]] to limit bridge rocking. [[Bass VI - The Loaf Pincher Home|The Loaf Pincher]] takes the more committed route: a hardtail conversion and a bridge modified so it cannot move freely. Those are separate changes, even though both aim for a steadier playing experience.
