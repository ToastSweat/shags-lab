# Star Wars Battlefront II

The Battlefront II discussed in these server notes is the classic 2005 game, not the later game with the same title. It puts players into large Star Wars battles with infantry, vehicles, and AI soldiers, making it a good candidate for a small group's cooperative PvE setup.

The local project is [[Star Wars Battlefront II Dedicated Server Home]], running under the name **PGN - Ewok Around and Find Out**. Its October 6, 2026 update reports automatic human-team assignment through [[Wiki/HK-47|HK-47]], with players placed on the Republic side for Clone Wars battles and the Empire side for Galactic Civil War battles. The documented goal is ordinary Steam/GOG clients joining without custom missions or client mods.

That working team enforcement is separate from the larger roadmap. Persistent PGN identities, profiles, XP, achievements, an economy, and a campaign are listed as future work, not finished features.

For the recent implementation story, see [[2026-10-06 - I Made HK-47 Assign The Teams]]. For why an old game acquired so much surrounding infrastructure, see [[Wiki/Progeny Gaming Network|Progeny Gaming Network]] and [[PGN - Game Eras and Server Genealogy]].
