# Garth Algar

Garth Algar is Wayne Campbell's friend and co-host in *Wayne's World*, played by Dana Carvey. He is the shy, enthusiastic, drum-playing half of the pair—the kind of character who makes sense as the center of an affectionate, thoroughly ridiculous side project.

Here he is the inspiration for [[Garths Game DMG Home|Garth's Game DMG]], a virtual-pet game being built as an actual Game Boy ROM. The project describes him as a burping and farting Tamagotchi. Its September 26, 2026 update puts Garth in a little industrial spaceship room, with food, entertainment, sleep, minigames, and persistent needs to manage.

That spaceship-and-pet-care setup belongs to the local game project; it is not a summary of the films' story. The development challenge is making the character feel alive within the Game Boy's limited graphics, sound, memory, and controls.

See [[2026-09-26.3 - I Swear I'm Going to Finish This Game!]] for the build diary and [[Wiki/Cosmic Raven Universe|Cosmic Raven Universe]] for the linked worldbuilding topic, whose exact connection to this game is not yet explained in the notes.
