# Progeny Gaming Network

Progeny Gaming Network, usually PGN, is the author's long-running gaming community. It formed around 2015 around [[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]] and Minecraft, then gradually became less of a formal clan and more of a persistent group of friends with an unfortunate willingness to host another server.

That is the useful distinction: PGN is the group, not one game or the old [[Wiki/PGN RPG|PGN RPG]] server. Its projects range from shared survival worlds such as [[Wiki/Valheim|Valheim]] and [[Wiki/Dune Awakening|Dune Awakening]] to a revived [[Wiki/Star Wars Battlefront II|Star Wars Battlefront II]] server and the [[Wiki/Safe Cracker|Safe Cracker]] Discord game.

The September 2026 Discord archive project gives this history an evidence trail. It preserves surviving messages from 2016 onward, while separating documented events from later memories and clan mythology. Early history—including [[Wiki/Hell Soldiers|Hell Soldiers]], [[Wiki/Helvete|Helvete]], and [[Wiki/Why So Serious RPG|Why So Serious RPG]]—needs that distinction, because the community predates the surviving Discord record.

Start at [[Progeny Gaming Network Home]] for the community overview or [[PGN Discord Archive Home]] for the research. [[PGN - The Epic History]] tells the longer story; [[PGN - Historical Evidence Ledger]] provides the supporting excerpts.
