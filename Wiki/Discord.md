# Discord

**Discord** is a communication platform organized around communities called servers, with text channels, voice chat, roles, and other shared spaces. A Discord server in that sense is a community, not necessarily a computer somebody personally hosts.

Here it appears in several different roles. [[Progeny Gaming Network Home|PGN]] uses it as a community space. The [[PGN Discord Archive Home|archive project]] preserves its history. [[Safe Cracker Discord Activity Home|Safe Cracker]] uses [[Wiki/Discord Activities|Discord Activities]] to let people play from inside Discord and share results.

Bots and integrations can connect Discord to other services through [[Wiki/API|APIs]], but permissions and [[Wiki/credentials|credentials]] still matter. A game shouldn't trust a browser's claimed identity merely because it launched inside Discord; the server needs to verify the login.

Keeping an archive also raises a different question from keeping a public website: what can be preserved privately isn't automatically appropriate to publish.
