# Obsidian

**Obsidian** is a note-taking app built around local [[Wiki/Markdown|Markdown]] files. A *vault* is the folder containing those notes and their attachments. The notes remain ordinary files rather than existing only inside an online service.

Its useful trick is connecting notes with links such as `[[QMK]]`. That turns an unfamiliar term in an update into a path toward an explanation, related project, or rebuild guide. The wiki pages here are meant to provide that context without turning every diary entry into a textbook.

The [[Obsidian Vault to Website Home|vault-to-website project]] uses this vault as the source and [[Wiki/Quartz|Quartz]] to build a public site. Its workflow selects notes marked `publish: true`; that selection is part of the publishing setup, not an automatic privacy guarantee provided by Markdown itself.

[[Wiki/Git|Git]] records changes, while the local notes remain the material worth preserving.
