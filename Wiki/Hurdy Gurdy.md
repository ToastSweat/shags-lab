# Hurdy Gurdy

A hurdy gurdy is a string instrument played with a crank and a set of keys. Turning the crank spins a rosined wheel against the strings, doing the continuous bowing job that a violinist normally does by hand. The keys change the melody notes, while other strings can keep a steady drone underneath.

That combination gives it the sustained, buzzing sound people often associate with folk or medieval music. It is not a little mechanical music box: the player still controls the crank, keys, and musical phrasing.

The local example is the [[Hurdy Gurdy - Nerdy Gurdy Home|Nerdy Gurdy BASIC kit]], documented as the four-string, 24-key version. Its project page describes an assemble-it-yourself instrument, with glue, tools, paint, and enthusiasm supplied by the builder. That makes it a different sort of instrument project from adjusting [[BASS VI - Penny Home|Penny]]: building the thing, rather than just sorting out its setup.
