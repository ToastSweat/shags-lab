## Marketing
This is the DiY kit for the Nerdy Gurdy BASIC, in the expanded version with 4 strings and 24 keys. It is based on the original Nerdy Gurdy design, but the sound and adjust-ability have been vastly improved, while making it also a lot easier to build. Due to the added capo’s you have the same functionality as the original 6-string instrument.

This instrument is very suited for beginning players to learn to play, as well as for advanced players who wish to have a robust instrument for e.g. traveling. It is recommended by many hurdy gurdy teachers and extensively used in music education in Europe.

The kit contains all the parts that you need to build a working instrument, including strings.  A detailed step-by-step manual is provided, and everything is designed to be easy to assemble (Ikea style). What you need to bring yourself are: glue, some basic tools, paint and enthusiasm.
