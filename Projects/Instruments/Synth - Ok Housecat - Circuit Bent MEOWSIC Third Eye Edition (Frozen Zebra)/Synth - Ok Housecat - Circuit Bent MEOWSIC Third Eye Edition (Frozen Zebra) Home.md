## Marketing
Behold: the THIRD EYE EDITION circuit bent cat piano! These Meowsic keyboards are purchased BRAND NEW and modified to allow for extreme sonic experimentation.  
  
mods include:  
SPEED CONTROL! Takes the cat from low growling purrs to manic high-pitched chirps  
LIGHT CONTROL! A light sensor is hidden within the cat's third eye, and when switched on allows the pitch to be controlled using light for a pseudo-theremin effect.  
AUDIO INPUT! An additional 1/4" jack has been added so that an external sound source can be plugged in and heard alongside the keyboard's sound, and will be affected by any fx that the keyboard is plugged into.  
  
Also includes:  
-1/4" output jack + volume knob  
-internal microphone toggle switch  
-custom 3d-printed knobs and hardware
