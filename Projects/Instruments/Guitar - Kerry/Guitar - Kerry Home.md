## Marketing
The B.C. Rich Kerry King Wartribe 1 is a stripped-down, aggressive take on the iconic Warlock shape, built to capture Kerry King's unmistakable visual style in a more affordable and straightforward package. This version pairs the savage Wartribe silhouette with a black finish and white/silver tribal graphics, giving it the menacing look associated with Kerry King's stage guitars while keeping the feature set direct and practical.

Its offset double-cutaway mahogany body uses pointed forward horns and the deeply notched lower body shape that make the Wartribe instantly recognizable. Combined with the matching Beast-style headstock, black hardware, and tribal graphic treatment, the guitar delivers a bold visual identity that fits squarely in the world of thrash and heavy metal.

A bolt-on maple neck and 25.5" scale length give the guitar a familiar, articulate feel, while the rosewood fingerboard and 24 frets provide plenty of room for aggressive riffing, fast lead work, and upper-register solos. The straightforward layout and simple control scheme make it easy to grab and go without overcomplicating things.

Two exposed B.C. Rich B.D.S.M. humbuckers provide the Wartribe 1 with a hot, heavy voice designed for distortion, chugging rhythms, and cutting leads. A three-way pickup selector and separate volume controls allow for some tonal flexibility, while a master tone control helps shape the overall response.

Unlike the earlier tremolo-equipped Wartribe variant, this version uses a string-through-body fixed bridge design, giving it a more direct feel, solid sustain, and simpler setup. That makes it especially appealing if you want the Kerry King look and attitude without the added maintenance of a locking tremolo system.
### Kerry King Tribal Styling
The visual centerpiece of this Wartribe 1 is its black finish with white/silver tribal graphics. The pattern gives the guitar the sharp, aggressive aesthetic associated with Kerry King's signature instruments while standing apart from the earlier red-fire version.

Together with the pointed Wartribe body and Beast-style headstock, the tribal graphics make this guitar look unmistakably built for metal.
### Mahogany Wartribe Body
The Wartribe 1 uses a mahogany body shaped in B.C. Rich's extreme offset double-cutaway style. Its pointed upper horns, centered lower cutaway, and angular lines give it a menacing stage presence.

Mahogany also provides a thick, solid tonal foundation, giving the guitar a little extra weight and body beneath the attack of its pickups.
### Maple Neck and Rosewood Fingerboard
 A bolt-on maple neck gives the guitar clarity, snap, and a familiar playing feel. The rosewood fingerboard carries 24 frets and dot inlays, offering a clean and functional playing surface with plenty of range for metal rhythm and lead work.

The 25.5" scale length provides traditional full-scale string tension, helping riffs stay tight and articulate.
### B.C. Rich B.D.S.M. Humbuckers
The Wartribe 1 is loaded with two exposed B.C. Rich B.D.S.M. humbuckers. These pickups are voiced for heavy music, delivering strong output and the kind of punchy response needed for distorted tones.

With two volume controls, one master tone, and a three-way pickup selector, the guitar keeps its controls simple while still allowing useful tonal adjustments.
### String-Through Fixed Bridge
This version of the Wartribe 1 uses a fixed Tune-o-matic-style bridge with string-through-body construction. Compared to a tremolo-equipped design, the fixed bridge offers a more solid feel, stable tuning, and easier restringing and setup.

That makes it a practical choice for players who want straightforward reliability along with the aggressive Kerry King aesthetic.
