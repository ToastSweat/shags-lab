# Preserved discussion notes

These are summaries of the conversation, not newly adopted rules. The founder chose to pause organizational design and start with personal experience.

## Core meanings

- Passion: what we care about, what drives us, and what shapes our lives and legacy.
- Strength: resolve and dedication; the will and desire to grow.
- Power: ability, developed through knowledge and skill.
- Victory: achieving chosen goals and overcoming obstacles.
- Broken chains: greater freedom from self-doubt, limiting dependence, and personal limitations.
- The Force: open to metaphorical and spiritual interpretation. Teachings develop through the Code, meditation, and lived experience.
- Balance: like water moving toward equilibrium. Light and darkness are deliberately open symbols, rather than fixed moral categories.
- Rest, realistic personal measures of growth, failure, and seeking help all belong within the path.

## Community vision

Belonging, meaning, spiritual exploration, shared imaginative expression, and support. Robes, candles, music, guided meditation, and discussion were envisioned for gatherings. Lightsaber classes could come much later. Intention is central to ritual; personal forms remain open.

The latest direction was welcoming: members and visitors may participate voluntarily, with no attendance or contribution quotas. Absence alone should not end membership. Reach out with care rather than demand justification. People should feel welcome to return or simply spend time together.

## Parked charter exploration

- Prospective membership as mutual exploration with a low barrier to entry.
- Proposed full-membership milestone: memorizing the original Sith Code, sustained participation, and personal commitment to growth.
- Admission by the Dark Lord or someone entrusted with that responsibility; ranks remain undefined.
- Freedom to question teachings and leaders, disagree, decline activities, and leave.
- Freedom to practice another faith was favored; its relationship to future legal recognition remains an unanswered research question.
- Departure should not depend on training a successor; leadership transitions can be arranged separately.
- Formal readmission and restoration of rank or office were discussed as individual decisions. Reconcile these ideas with the later, more welcoming direction before adopting anything.
- Earlier strict hiatus and disengagement-review proposals were superseded by the welcoming approach above.
- Address conflict through conversation, boundaries, and practical separation when needed. Suspension or expulsion as last resorts, with immediate protection possible for serious circumstances.
- Leaders accountable for abuse, exploitation, or serious misconduct. Fair review, succession, authority, and decision-making processes remain undeveloped.
- Earned titles, ceremonial robes, names, and lightsabers were explored; no progression system was finalized.

## Personal ritual possibilities

The founder is interested in robes, candles, incense, runes, and mysticism. A lightsaber might direct intention, mark ritual space, or accompany slowed movements inspired by lightsaber forms, perhaps with a tai chi-like pace. These are possibilities to explore, not established practices or claims of supernatural effects.

## The present decision

“I will walk the path I want others to walk.”

Begin as the founder learning what the path means. Avoid inventing elaborate practices merely to fill a system. Record experience in a founder’s journal; another person may eventually join the journey.
