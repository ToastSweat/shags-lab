_version 0.1 - 11/2/2026_

The Sith Order is a spiritual community founded on passion, personal growth, and the pursuit of freedom. Drawing inspiration from the Sith of the Star Wars universe and the Sith Code, we seek to develop the resolve, understanding, and ability to shape our own lives.

We believe that passion gives our mortal lives meaning. What we love, pursue, create, and care for helps define who we are and what we leave behind. Each person must discover those passions for themselves.

Life involves struggle, and **peace** is a fleeting state. We understand the Force through balance: like water, it flows toward equilibrium. Light and darkness, rest and effort, are parts of that understanding. We must replenish the energy we expend, allowing both striving and restoration a place in our lives.

We pursue personal growth with dedication and realistic expectations, measuring progress according to our own circumstances and chosen goals. **Passion** drives us. Rest sustains us. Failure teaches us. Independence is cherished, but asking for help is not weakness. It is an expression of responsibility and strength.

We understand **strength** as resolve and dedication: the will to grow. We understand **power** as ability: the knowledge and skill to act. Through applying that ability, we achieve personal victories, overcome obstacles, and expand our **freedom**.

Our ambitions carry responsibility. Passion, rank, and ability never excuse cruelty or exploitation. We recognize the need for self-defense, firm boundaries, and difficult truths, while practicing empathy and consideration toward others. Trust is earned through conduct and cherished when given.

The Force offers a language for exploring life, connection, and possibility. Members may understand it metaphorically or spiritually. Our teachings develop through reflection on the Sith Code, meditation, and lived experience. We approach what we do not understand with curiosity and humility.

The Order offers companionship in that exploration. Through gathering, discussion, learning, creativity, and ritual, we support one another in finding meaning and pursuing growth. Intention gives ritual its significance, and personal practice may take many forms. Our shared symbols, robes, names, and ceremonies give expression to our commitments and belonging.

Recognition within the Order develops through demonstrated dedication. The first milestone includes memorization of the Sith Code, sustained participation, and a personal commitment to growth. Further ranks and symbols will have clearly defined meanings and expectations.

The Dark Lord of the Sith guides the Order and remains accountable to its principles. Members retain the freedom to question teachings, challenge leaders, and express disagreement. Personal respect is earned through conduct. Serious abuse, exploitation, or misconduct **are grounds for removal from leadership through a fair process.**

We undertake this path as human beings who will sometimes struggle, fail, and need one another. We commit to learning, trying again, and helping build a community in which passion can flourish.

We are dedicated fans in capes, and we are also people sincerely committed to bettering ourselves. Our inspiration is imaginative; our intentions are serious. We ask for the freedom to practice our beliefs and express ourselves, while respecting that others may not share them. Through our actions, we seek to earn recognition and demonstrate that the Order can be a helpful resource to people throughout the wider human community.
