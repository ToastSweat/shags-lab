_Working draft This expresses the Order’s interpretation of the original Code._

**“Peace is a lie, there is only passion.”**

Life offers no permanent freedom from struggle. Peace is a fleeting state, and challenges offer opportunities to learn and grow. We seek balance, allowing striving and restoration a place in our lives.

Passion gives our mortal lives meaning. What we love, pursue, create, and care for helps define who we are. Through our actions, creations, and influence on others, we leave something of those passions behind.

**“Through passion, I gain strength.”**

What we care about gives us a reason to grow. Passion nurtures strength: the resolve and dedication to pursue that growth, face difficulty, and try again after failure.

Strength includes recognizing when we need rest, another perspective, or help. We pursue our commitments with realistic expectations and consideration for ourselves.

**“Through strength, I gain power.”**

Through dedication and sustained effort, we develop ability. We learn, practice, reflect, and gain the knowledge and skill to act.

Power is that ability: the capacity to put our intentions into practice. Its development carries responsibility. It grants no entitlement to exploit or control others.

**“Through power, I gain victory.”**

By applying our ability, we accomplish chosen goals and overcome obstacles. Victory takes its meaning from the individual’s pursuits and circumstances.

Each person sets realistic measures of achievement. A victory may be a major accomplishment or a small step that once felt beyond reach.

**“Through victory, my chains are broken.”**

Our achievements can loosen self-doubt, expand what we are capable of, and reduce dependence that restricts our choices. Through these victories, we gain greater freedom to shape our lives.

Freedom can include choosing support and connection. Independence and belonging can strengthen one another.

**“The Force shall free me.”**

Through dedication to the Force, its teachings, and the Sith Order, we pursue freedom from personal limitations. Those teachings develop through the philosophy of the Code, meditation, and lived experience.

The Force may hold metaphorical or spiritual meaning for each member. Like water flowing toward equilibrium, it offers an image of balance. Light and darkness remain open symbols through which members may explore their own understanding.

This dedication is a continuing practice of reflection, growth, and participation. Through it, we develop the understanding and ability to direct our own lives.
