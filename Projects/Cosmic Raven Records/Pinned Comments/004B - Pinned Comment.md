# 004B - Pinned Comment

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K2G7XC_31I8)

Coming back to this after spending more time with the receiver. I think I was still making the wrong thing important.

I was obsessed with which version was better. Which one was mine. Whether borrowing from one of them counted as stealing.

I’m not saying those questions stopped mattering. But I went back through my notes from 004A and 004B.

The settings weren’t as identical as I thought.

One of the television controls was slightly off. Barely enough to notice. At the time I would have called that meaningless. I don’t anymore.

I’ve started learning that tiny changes can move the receiver much farther than they should.

So maybe I wasn’t choosing between two versions of the same song. Maybe I was looking at two completely different places.

I’m starting to think the dial is only part of the tuning.
