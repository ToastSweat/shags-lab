I said I was going back to 001.

That took longer than I expected.

The problem was the television. It was useful when I added it, but after spending this much time with the receiver I realized it was only showing me the crudest version of what was happening.

So I tore part of the setup apart and rebuilt the display.

I can see the signal much more clearly now.

I'm not sure that's the same thing as understanding it.

I've started saving those patterns with the recordings.

I don't know what they mean yet.

But the receiver isn't just giving me cleaner audio now. For the first time, I feel like I can actually watch it work.

Now I'm ready to go back to 001.
