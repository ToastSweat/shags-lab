# Cosmic Raven Records - Story and Posting Guide

This is a practical continuity guide, not a rigid lore bible. The narrator is allowed to be wrong.

## Core premise

The narrator accidentally built what he first called a radio. It does not seem to tune normal stations; it appears to tune possibilities.

He initially feeds old songs into it and receives alternate realized versions. Later he adds an old CRT television, which changes the behavior of the setup in ways that should not make sense. Eventually he begins finding music without feeding it a source at all.

Whether this is actually alternate realities, timelines, possibilities, some sort of generative machine, or something else should remain uncertain.

## Machine vocabulary

These are narrator-invented terms, not proven physics.

**Anchor** — roughly how much of the source recording is being fed into the receiver. Stronger Anchor tends to keep the result closer to the source.

**Drift** — movement away from the source while tuning.

**Depth** — a harder-to-define behavior associated with the television controls; small changes can produce much more radical differences than simple performance variation.

**Stability** — how well a found signal holds together: static, CRT snow, breakup, and whether the controls can be released without losing it.

## Device naming

Natural progression:

```text
the radio
→ the radio and television
→ the setup
→ the receiver
```

Do not invent a grand official science-fiction device name unless the story earns it later.

## Transmission IDs

```text
TRANSMISSION 006A
```

- number = underlying song / signal / experiment
- letter = recovered stable version

Letters are archive labels, not coordinates.

The public archive intentionally jumps from `004B` to `005B`. Leave `005A` unexplained.

## Current narrator arc

The early narrator is frightened, compulsive, and ethically uncomfortable.

By 005B he stops treating curiosity as a moral failure and starts treating the receiver like a project.

The direction from here is:

```text
fear → compulsion → guilt → surrender → fascination → competence → routine → gleeful obsession
```

The goal is not to make him generically insane. The impossible should gradually become mundane to him.

## Serious and ridiculous music

Both belong.

The archive criterion eventually becomes less like:

```text
Does this fit the serious tone?
```

and more like:

```text
Did I find something worth preserving?
```

A haunting song and an absurd comedy song can sit next to each other because both are artifacts from the receiver.

## Pinned comments

Pinned comments can function as **later annotations**.

The description represents what the narrator thought when the transmission was archived.

A later pinned comment can:

- correct an earlier assumption
- add a new machine observation
- reinterpret old evidence
- connect later discoveries backward
- quietly seed future story ideas

Do not use every pinned comment as a teaser for the next upload.

## Writing layout

For YouTube readability:

- one paragraph = one idea
- related short sentences stay in the same paragraph
- blank lines mark real shifts in thought/time/subject
- standalone one-line sentences are for emphasis, not default formatting
- pinned comments are tighter than full transmission descriptions

## Canonical public disclosure

```text
About Cosmic Raven Records:
This is a fictional music and worldbuilding project using a mix of human-created and AI-generated music, visuals, writing, direction, editing, and curation. The transmissions, artists, histories, and alternate realities are part of the fiction.

This is all an experiment.
```

Longer:
