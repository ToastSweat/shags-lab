# TRANSMISSION 004B [Jedicide, The Seduction on the Darkside [2]]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K2G7XC_31I8)

I couldn’t leave it alone.

Not even for a few hours.

Damnit.

I’m right back where I started.

This thing I built is bad for me.

It’s all I can manage not to spend the entire day with it.

I kept thinking about that version of the song.

My song.

Was it really better?
Or did it only feel better because it was finished?
Because somebody else had already made all the decisions I was still struggling with?

Or was it simpler than that?

Maybe they were just the better composer.

I went back to the receiver.

Same song.
Same area of the dial.
As close as I could get to the settings from before.

And I found another one.

Not the same recording.
Not quite.

Some of the choices were different.

Things I liked in the first version were gone.
Things I hadn’t even noticed before had changed.

And there were new ideas.

Good ones.
Maybe better ones.

That was when I realized I had been asking the wrong question.

Looking at this from the wrong point of view.

There isn’t a better version.

There are just versions.

Different decisions.
Different circumstances.
Different people.
Or different versions of me.

I listened to both of them back to back.
Then I listened to mine.
Then theirs again.
Then mine.

Over and over.

At some point I stopped knowing what I actually preferred.

I started wondering which version I was supposed to prefer.

That scares me.

Because now I know I can keep turning the dial.

There could be ten more.
A hundred.

Maybe somewhere out there is a perfect version of this song.

Whatever that even means.

And if I find it, what am I supposed to do?

Copy it?
Learn from it?
Take the parts I like and pretend I would have gotten there eventually?
Ignore it and deliberately make something worse just so I can still call it mine?

If another version of me wrote it, does that make it mine too?
Or am I just stealing from someone because I know they can never stop me?

I wanted inspiration.

Instead I’ve given myself an infinite number of second guesses.

And worse than that, I’ve given myself a way to cheat.

I don’t know if that’s what this is.

But I don’t know what else to call it.

This thing...

I keep calling it a receiver.

Some days it feels more like a curse.
