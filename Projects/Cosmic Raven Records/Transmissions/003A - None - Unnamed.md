# TRANSMISSION 003A [None, Unnamed]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MU_QGWQwVSY)

I think I’ve reached the limit of what I can do with the radio alone.

I’ve gotten better at finding signals. Or maybe I’ve just gotten better at recognizing what I’m looking for.

There’s one control I’ve started thinking of as the Anchor.

It’s really just the amount of the original recording I’m feeding into the radio, but the name seems appropriate. The stronger the source, the closer everything stays to what I remember.

Turn it down and things begin to... 'drift'.

I’ve started writing down the positions of the dials. Frequencies. Input levels. Anything that might help me reproduce a result.

It helps.
Sometimes.

The problem is that hearing the signal isn’t enough anymore.

There are moments where I know something is there, buried underneath the static, but I can’t tell what I’m actually doing to it. I can turn the dial until the sound changes, but that doesn’t tell me why it changed.

So I added a television.

An old CRT. Nothing special.

The original idea was just to give myself something visual to look at while I was working. Feed the signal through it, watch the snow and interference move while I tuned the radio.

I thought maybe I could see patterns, some sort of visual indicator. Learn to recognize a good signal before I could hear it clearly.

That isn’t what happened.

The television must have changed the signal.
I don’t know how else to explain it.

Controls that should have absolutely nothing to do with the audio are affecting what comes through the radio.

Not just the quality of the reception.
The song itself.

I spent most of the night convincing myself I had wired something incorrectly, because I probably did.

But that doesn’t explain what I heard.

This transmission came from one of my old songs again.

At least, that’s where it started.

I’m beginning to think calling this thing a radio isn’t going to make sense for much longer, but I really don't know what to call it anymore.
