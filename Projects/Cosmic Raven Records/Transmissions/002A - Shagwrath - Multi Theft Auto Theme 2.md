# TRANSMISSION 002A [Shagwrath, Multi Theft Auto Theme [2]]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uKdTXT9Hn4M)

I caved.

The allure of the unknown, and this morbid curiosity, won out over better judgment.

I keep telling myself that maybe this is a good thing. Maybe I made something extraordinary. Maybe I owe it to the world to share what I’m hearing.

But I can’t shake the feeling that this is dangerous.

All great technology can be dangerous in the wrong hands. But this feels different.

It feels like a drug.
I keep coming back to it.
I want more.

I’ve listened to alternate versions of songs I know. Old songs. New songs. Bands I grew up with. Things I thought I understood completely.

Sometimes the differences are obvious.
Sometimes they’re almost nothing.

A different choice in a melody. A vocal inflection. A guitar tone. A pause that lasts half a second longer.

And somehow those are the ones that get me the most.

I’ve spent dozens of hours turning the dials, trying to understand how this thing works. Trying to map it. Trying to find some pattern.

Maybe there is not one at all.
Maybe it’s just too complicated for me.
I’m just one man staring at something much bigger than I am.

I keep finding myself listening to multiple versions of the same song, trying to decide which one is better.

Worse than that, I catch myself wondering which one is closer to the version I would have made.

That thought bothers me more than I want to admit.

Maybe it’s better to distract myself with nostalgia.

Maybe I should be the martyr here. Keep the machine to myself. Keep everyone else safe from it.

Because I’m starting to wonder if the danger isn’t what the machine can show us.

Maybe the danger is how badly we want to keep looking.
