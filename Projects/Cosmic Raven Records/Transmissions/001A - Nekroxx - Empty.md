# TRANSMISSION 001A [Nekroxx, Empty]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3JIIzlHZgMQ)

I built a... 'radio'. That’s the best thing to call it.

But it doesn’t seem to tune into places. It tunes into possibilities.

I know that sounds crazy, and I don’t know if I believe it myself yet, but I think I’m hearing what could have been. An alternate reality, I guess.

I gave it a song I wrote years ago-half finished, never fully realized. Like so many others. Something I knew well enough that I’d recognize it immediately.

I turned the dial.

Somewhere, somehow, somebody else was playing it.

This isn’t my version.

This is theirs.

Or maybe it’s the version that could have been.
