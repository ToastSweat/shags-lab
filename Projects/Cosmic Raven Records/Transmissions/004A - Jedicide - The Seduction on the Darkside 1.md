# TRANSMISSION 004A [Jedicide, The Seduction on the Darkside [1]]

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4wBkw9Awo9M)

I tried to stay away.

I really did.

I had already wasted too much time listening to things that never happened. Songs I knew. Songs I didn’t. Familiar bands playing unfamiliar versions of familiar music.

And since adding the television, things have gotten stranger.

Some of what I’ve found is beautiful.
Some of it is... bizarre.
Some of it I genuinely don’t know what to make of, or if I can even call it music.

At some point I realized I was spending more time listening to what other versions of the world had made than actually making anything myself. That really bothered me.

So I decided to put it down for a while and to invest my energy into something productive. I went back through my old projects, old half finished songs and recording. Ideas from twenty years ago that never became what I thought they could be.

I started cleaning them up.
Rewriting things.
Finishing things.

Actually making music again instead of sitting in front of that machine lamenting and wasting time wondering what might have been.

It felt good.
i felt like I was doing better than ever.

Then the curiosity came back, and it grew stronger and stronger until I just couldn't resist anymore.

Maybe I was right before.
Maybe this thing really is a drug.
Maybe it is dangerous.

I told myself I was only going to listen for a few minutes.

This time I didn’t feed it anything.
No recording.
No anchor.

I just started tuning.

And I heard something familiar.

It took me a few seconds to understand why.

It was the song I had been working on.

Not an old version.
Not something I had already recorded and forgotten.

The song I was working on now.

Except it was finished.
Fully realized.

And better.

At least, I thought it was better.

That should have been exciting.

Instead, it scared the hell out of me.

Because the more I listened, the more I realized something else.

It was closer to my version than these signals usually are.

Not identical.
But close.

And everything I had changed in my own song over the last few days seemed to be pushing it closer to what I was hearing through the receiver.

So which direction is this going?

Am I finding a version of the song that already exists somewhere else?
Or am I changing what I find by changing mine?

Was this always out there?
Did my work somehow pull me closer to it?
Or is the machine just showing me what I want to hear?

I don’t know which answer bothers me more.

And now I have another problem.

Because I heard things in that version that I want to use.

Ideas I never would have thought of.
Choices that make the song better.

But are they mine?

If another version of me wrote them, does that count?
Would taking them be inspiration?
Stealing?

Does it even matter if the person I’m stealing from is me?

I wanted this machine to show me what could have been.

I didn’t expect it to make me question what I’m allowed to make now.
