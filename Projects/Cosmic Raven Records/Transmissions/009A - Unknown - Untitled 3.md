# TRANSMISSION 009A [Unknown, Untitled 3]

I think I made a mistake calling it static.

I went back to the area where I found 007A and 008A. Anchor at zero. Same general range. This time, instead of chasing the first thing that sounded musical, I left the controls alone for a while and listened.

There was more in there than I realized.

Not one signal.

Several.

They weren't clean enough to separate at first. Just fragments surfacing and disappearing into each other. A drum pattern for a few seconds. A synth line underneath it. Something else trying to occupy the same space.

I've probably heard this before.

I just thought it was noise.

Once I started making smaller adjustments to Depth, one of the signals began to pull away from the others. I backed off the Drift, waited for the picture to settle, and eventually got it by itself.

That's this recording.

Another unknown song. Another unknown artist.

And again, it feels like it belongs near the last two.

Not the same song. Not the same performer. Maybe not even the same kind of music exactly.

But close enough that I think I can steer toward this sound now.

That's useful in a way I wasn't expecting.

I'm not just searching anymore.

I'm starting to choose what I want to look for.

The part I haven't figured out is what the other signals were.

I didn't move very far to find this one.

And for a few seconds, before I isolated it, I could hear at least three things trying to come through at once.

Maybe more.

I used to think the empty spaces on the dial were empty.

I don't think that anymore.
