# TRANSMISSION 007A [Unknown, Untitled 1]

I finally stopped asking the receiver to show me something I already knew.

After 006, it felt like the obvious thing to try.

No source recording. Anchor at zero. Nothing for the receiver to follow back to me.

I've done that before, accidentally or otherwise, and it still managed to land on something familiar. So this time I kept moving.

More Drift than I usually allow. Small changes to the television. Let the picture roll. Let the audio disappear completely and then worked backward whenever something started pushing through the static.

For a while it was nothing.

Then there was a pulse.

A few notes. Drums underneath it. Something electronic, but heavier than most of what I've been finding.

I overshot it twice.

The third time I got the picture to hold long enough to bring the audio in clean.

And then I just sat there listening.

I kept waiting to recognize it.

A melody. A lyric. A riff. Anything that would connect it to something I've written, something I've heard, or one of the signals I've already recorded.

Nothing.

As far as I can tell, this has nothing to do with me.

That sounds obvious now that I've written it down, but it changes something.

Until tonight I could still tell myself the receiver was doing something to the material I gave it. Rearranging it. Extrapolating from it. Pulling possibilities out of things that were already there.

I don't think I can use that explanation anymore.

There was no source.

There was just a song.

Someone made this.

Somewhere.

Or somewhen.

Or whatever word actually belongs here.

I don't know who they are.

I don't know where this came from.

I don't even know if asking those questions the way I keep asking them makes sense.

But for the first time, I don't think I found another version of one of my songs.

I think I found somebody else's.

Holy shit.

I found somebody.
