# TRANSMISSION 001B-A [Nekroxx, Empty]

I went back to the beginning.

Or at least I tried to.

001 was the first signal I ever managed to hold long enough to record. Back then I had a radio, a half-finished song, and absolutely no idea what I was doing.

No television.

No Anchor, Drift, Depth, or Stability.

No real notes.

I just turned the dial until something impossible happened.

I thought that meant I had found a place.

Now I know better.

Getting back into the same general area wasn't actually that difficult. The old notes were almost useless, but I still had the original recording, and that gave me enough of an Anchor to work from.

The new display changed everything.

The old television mostly told me whether the signal was there.

This shows me movement inside it.

Even with the audio locked and the picture stable, the pattern never stops. It keeps pulling forward, folding, stretching, narrowing, opening again. I don't know if I'm watching the signal change or watching the receiver move through it.

Maybe neither.

But I can see differences now that I would never have noticed when I recorded 001A.

And there are more of them than I expected.

I found Empty again.

Not the recording I released.

Another version.

Close enough that I knew exactly what I was hearing, but different in the way 006 taught me to recognize immediately.

That alone wouldn't surprise me anymore.

What surprised me was the display.

There were other structures moving around it.

Other paths. Other patterns. Some close enough to overlap before separating again.

I spent a long time trying to decide whether I was looking at interference.

Then I remembered how long I spent calling everything I didn't understand "static."

So I'm trying not to make that mistake twice.

I thought I was returning to the first signal I ever found.

I'm starting to think there was never a "first signal."

There was just the first one I managed to hear.

This is another one.
