## Titles that need human judgment

### Clan Troll

The original idea — somebody who has been around forever, contributed little in conventional terms, but supplied huge amounts of jokes, insults and trolling — is exactly the kind of title a machine should **not** decide alone.

A script can count swear words, joke markers, reactions and project links.

It cannot reliably distinguish affectionate insult from hostility, deadpan humor from seriousness, or contribution that happened entirely in voice chat.

The correct workflow is a shortlist plus clan argument.

### Necromancer
Who most often revives dead games, servers or ideas?

### Patron Saint of Bad Ideas
This needs stories, not counts.

### The "One More Server" Award
Who is most responsible for the thought "should we make a server?"

### Lorekeeper
Who remembers old players, old server details and forgotten events everybody else lost?

### Professional Disappearing Act
Better nominated by the clan than inferred from text gaps.

## Recommended process

Generate 3–5 candidates per title, post the evidence in Discord, let everyone argue, then preserve the winning explanation with the title.

The story is the award.
