# Progeny Gaming Network [PGN]
_est. 2015_

### Who We Are:
Progeny Gaming Network is a long-running gaming community built around playing games, hosting servers, hanging out, and generally causing problems together.

### A Brief History of Bad Decisions:
PGN formed around 2015 and has bounced between a ridiculous number of games over the years. Some lasted a weekend, some lasted years, and several are now preserved in the [[Wiki/Discord|Discord]] **Graveyard** where they belong.

PGN started out as a dedicated clan for **[[Wiki/Multi Theft Auto|Multi Theft Auto]]** and **Minecraft**. Since then, there have been countless projects, servers, and good times. These days we're more of a group of friends than a formal clan, but every now and then the clan still picks up somebody new.

### What We Do:
- Play games together *(when there's anything to actually play)*
- Host community game servers *(mostly for our own benefit, but we are considering expanding into the good of mankind once more)*
- Share screenshots, clips, mods, and nonsense *(nut)*
- Occasionally resurrect games nobody has touched in ten years *(to mixed success)*
